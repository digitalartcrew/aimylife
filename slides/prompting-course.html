Conceptual Architecture

Micro-Level vs. Macro-Level AI

Micro-Level: Powerful Phrases

Refining single queries to set clear reasoning steps, negative constraints, and output personas.

"Think step-by-step. Focus exclusively on revenue."

Macro-Level: Prompt Chaining

Connecting multiple steps in sequence. The output of Step A becomes the input of Step B.

Step 1: Ideate → Step 2: Plan → Step 3: Localize
5-Step Framework • Part 1

Step 1 & Step 2: Establish & Invert

Step 1: Define Initial Task

Start a new chat to set your high-level objective:

"I want to learn a new skill to [your goal], but I'm not sure where to start. Help me brainstorm some ideas."

Step 2: Meta-Prompting

Ask AI how to refine its own thinking:

"What questions can I answer to help you tailor your suggestions?"
5-Step Framework • Part 2

Step 3 & Step 4: Context & Persona

Step 3: Provide Details

Answer the AI's diagnostic questions in the same chat:

"Here are some details about me: - [Answer 1] - [Answer 2] Based on this, suggest [three] skills relevant to me."

Step 4: Define a Persona

Adopt an expert evaluation perspective:

"Now, act as a [career coach / hiring manager]. Which of these skills should I focus on first? Explain your reasoning."
5-Step Framework • Part 3

Step 5: Decide on Next Steps

Convert open advice into a concrete, structured action plan.

The Action-Oriented Prompt Template

"Suggest [three] actions I can take [this week] to start learning that skill. Format this as a [table] with columns: [Action, Time Required, Expected Deliverable]."

Pro Tip: Always prompt the AI: "Please ask me any follow-up or clarifying questions before continuing."

Precision Prompting

Guiding AI with Powerful Phrases

Provide a Process

"Think step-by-step." "First [action 1]. Second [action 2]. Finally [action 3]."

Set Hard Boundaries

"Focus exclusively on [Topic A] and do not mention [Topic B]." "Write a one-paragraph summary."

Define Audience & Tone

"Write this for an audience of [executives]. The tone should be [objective and professional]."

Request a Critique

"Play devil's advocate. What is the strongest counterargument to this proposal?"
Iterative Depth

Expand, Elaborate, and Pivot

Generate Alternatives

Broaden your options before deciding:

"Give me 3 different versions of this." "What is an unconventional alternative approach to solving this problem?"

Targeted Expansion

Drill down into specific weak spots:

"Elaborate on point 2." "Provide more detail and specific examples for the section about [topic]."
Advanced Workflow

Prompt Chaining: The Assembly Line

Tackle large projects by passing outputs directly into subsequent prompts.

  • 1
    Prompt 1 (Ideation): "I'm going to Paris for 3 days. I like art and parks. Suggest a few well-known places to visit."
  • 2
    Prompt 2 (Optimization): "Using those locations, create a logical day-by-day itinerary that minimizes travel time."
  • 3
    Prompt 3 (Refinement): "For each day of the itinerary, suggest a few restaurants located near the afternoon destinations."

Thread Hygiene

When to Reset: Context Management

Long chats accumulate noise and context decay. Reset cleanly without losing progress.

The Context Preservation Prompt

Before closing or restarting a chat session, type:

"Summarize our discussion so far into key decisions, parameters, and resolved items."

Copy the summary directly into your new session to restart with a clean memory slate.
