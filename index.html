Who we are

A community nonprofit, not a bootcamp.

Most people's first encounter with AI is a chatbot window and no idea what to type. Most people's second encounter is a $2,000 course they never finish. AI My Life exists for the gap in between — practical instruction, in a room with other beginners, free.

We teach four tracks, each built around what people actually need: running a household, running a small business, earning money with a new skill, and passing the Google AI certification. Every session is live, every session is recorded, and every recording lands in the student portal within a day.

Over 85% of people who enroll in a self-paced technical certificate abandon it. A standing weekly hour with other humans is the single cheapest fix for that, and it is the whole idea behind this organization.

What you get, every week

  • A live hour on Zoom — taught, not pre-recorded, with time for your actual questions.
  • The recording, posted to the student portal usually the same day.
  • Slides and worksheets — prompt packs, templates, planners you keep.
  • Calendar invites and reminders at 24 hours and 1 hour before, automatically.
  • A cohort — the same faces each week, which is the part that makes you finish.

Questions

Before you register.

Is it really free?

Yes. We are a nonprofit and the workshops are free to attend. If you want to support the program there are pay-what-you-can options, but nothing is gated behind them — every student gets the same sessions, recordings, and materials.

I have never used an AI tool. Is this too advanced?

No — the Personal Life track assumes zero experience and starts with getting an assistant set up on your phone. Most people in the room are in their first few weeks with these tools.

What if I cannot make 6 AM HST?

Every session is recorded and posted to the student portal, usually the same day, along with the slides and worksheets. You will not fall behind by missing a week — but the live hour is where you get your own questions answered, so come when you can.

Why does my track only meet once a month?

There is one live hour a week and four tracks, so the tracks take turns — Personal Life, Small Business, Generating Revenue, then Certification, and around again. Between your track's sessions you have the recording, the slides, and the worksheets in the portal, and you are welcome to sit in on the other tracks' weeks.

Can I join more than one track?

Yes — and because the tracks rotate rather than run at the same time, there is no clash. Register for the one you care about most, then email us and we will add you to the others. All tracks share the same student portal.

Do I need to buy an AI subscription?

No. Everything we teach works on the free tiers of the major assistants. Where a paid plan genuinely changes what is possible we will say so, and say whether it is worth it.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop or phone, a Zoom connection, and one real task you would like to hand off. The sessions are hands-on — you build on your own machine while we go.
